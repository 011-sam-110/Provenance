## Earth renders (2)

Files: `public/marketing/landing/`.

| File | Shows |
|---|---|
| `still-hero.webp` | The Earth half lit, seen from 3.75 Earth radii, centred on 40° W, 22° N. The hero opens on it. |
| `still-whole.webp` | The whole sunlit Earth, centred on 15° W, 12° N. The page closes on it. |

Both were made for this project in Blender 5.1.2 (Cycles) on 2026-10-04, and each is a
1,600 px square WebP with a transparent ground. They are renders of a model, not
photographs. The model is a sphere textured with the NASA imagery below:

| Used for | Image | File |
|---|---|---|
| Day side | Blue Marble Next Generation, topography and bathymetry, October 2004, 5400 x 2700. Visible Earth record 73826. | https://eoimages.gsfc.nasa.gov/images/imagerecords/73000/73826/world.topo.bathy.200410.3x5400x2700.jpg |
| City lights on the night side | Black Marble 2016 colour map, 3 km. Visible Earth record 144898. | https://eoimages.gsfc.nasa.gov/images/imagerecords/144000/144898/BlackMarble_2016_3km.jpg |
| Clouds | Blue Marble cloud composite, 2048 x 1024. Visible Earth record 57747. | https://eoimages.gsfc.nasa.gov/images/imagerecords/57000/57747/cloud_combined_2048.jpg |

All three file URLs answered `200 image/jpeg` on 2026-10-05. The record pages at
`visibleearth.nasa.gov/images/<record>` now redirect to the Earth Observatory home page, so
the file URLs are the stable reference.

Two more inputs are not NASA's. The ocean glint uses a land mask made from Natural Earth
110m country outlines (public domain). The night side also carries a glow layer of dots:
the positions in this project's own map snapshot of 2026-10-04. Those dots are baked into
the render. They stay at 2026-10-04 when `scripts/gen-landing-snapshot.mjs` refreshes the
snapshot that the page draws over the render.

**NASA's terms.** NASA does not claim copyright in this imagery, which is why it is called
public domain. Quoted from https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/:

> NASA content – images, audio, video, and media files used in the rendition of
> 3-dimensional models, such as texture maps and polygon data in any format – generally are
> not subject to copyright in the United States.

> NASA should be acknowledged as the source of the material.

> If the NASA material is to be used for commercial purposes, including advertisements, it
> must not explicitly or implicitly convey NASA's endorsement of commercial goods or
> services.

**Credit line.** Use this wherever the renders are shown:

> Earth imagery: NASA Earth Observatory, NASA Visible Earth (Blue Marble Next Generation,
> Black Marble 2016). Rendered in Blender.

The same page says the NASA insignia and logotype are not in the public domain. Do not add
them beside the credit.

## Adding an image

1. Download it through the Pexels tool, not from a raw image URL, so that a ledger row with
   the creator, the page URL and the sha256 exists from the first moment.
2. Put the file under `public/` and serve it from this site. Do not hot-link it.
3. Add its row to this file in the same change.

An image that is not from Pexels needs its own section here: who made it, the terms it is
under, and the URL where a reader can check both.
